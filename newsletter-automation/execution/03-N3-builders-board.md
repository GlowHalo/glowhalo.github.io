## 창간호 초안

**제목**: Welcome to The Builder's Board — Should You Actually Build That AI Feature?

---

Every AI builder has the same failure mode: you get excited about what's technically possible and
skip the question of whether anyone's waiting for it. This newsletter runs the experiment
differently — every week, one real "should I build this" dilemma gets torn apart by three
independent AI perspectives who never see each other's answers first.

**This week's board**

Dilemma: *"I built a Chrome extension that summarizes long Slack threads with GPT-4. Should I
charge $5/month, or keep it free with a paid Pro tier later?"* (a genuinely common launch decision
for solo AI builders, chosen because it's instructive, not because it has one right answer)

- **Market verdict — CROWDED BUT SEGMENTABLE.** Generic "AI summarizer" tools are saturated, but
  Slack-specific, workflow-embedded tools have a real wedge — the competition is broad AI apps,
  not Slack-native ones. Charging immediately filters for real intent and gives you paying-user
  feedback instead of vanity installs.
- **Tech verdict — LOW RISK, LOW MOAT.** A GPT-4 API wrapper has near-zero technical risk to
  build, which also means near-zero technical moat — anyone can clone the core feature in a
  weekend. Your defensibility has to come from distribution and workflow depth, not the AI call
  itself.
- **Distribution verdict — SLACK APP DIRECTORY IS THE REAL PRODUCT.** The extension itself is a
  distribution channel, not just a feature — getting listed and ranking in Slack's own app
  directory matters more than any outbound marketing you'll do in month one.

**Composite: Charge from day one (even a small amount) — free-with-upsell-later mostly trains
users to expect free. Put your energy into the Slack App Directory listing, not a landing page.**
